## 3. How the Game works

- **Lobbies.** A host creates a lobby and shares a code, link, or QR code. Players join with a display name and, optionally, an Instagram handle. Anyone with the code or link can join. Share it only with people you intend to play with.
- **Location sharing.** During a game your device sends your live GPS location to our server. Depending on your role, other players see some of your location. For example, hunters receive hiders' locations at each "ping," which happens about every 60 seconds by default, plus at certain power-up events. Your display name, Instagram handle (if provided), role, and in-game status are visible to the other players in your lobby.
- **Recaps.** At the end of a round, players in the lobby can see a recap that may include each player's location trail on a map.
- **Optional features.** You may optionally consent to a one-time camera snapshot (the "hider cam sneak peek"), record video on your own device, and send short highlight clips to the host. Our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) explains how these work.
- **No accounts, no saved games.** Games live only in server memory. A game may be lost at any time, for example when the server restarts, sleeps, or a lobby closes.

## 6. Recording other people

The Game lets you record video and audio on your own device, and it can capture a camera snapshot. **You are solely responsible for anything you record and for how you use or share it.**

- **Get consent.** Before you record other players or bystanders, ask whether they're okay with it. Stop if anyone objects.
- **Follow recording laws.** Recording laws vary by location. **California generally requires the consent of all parties** to record a confidential communication, including audio of a private conversation (California Penal Code § 632). Other states and countries have their own rules. Don't record audio of people's conversations without their consent.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't record in places where people expect privacy, such as restrooms, changing areas, or inside homes. Don't record children without a parent's consent.
- **Sharing.** Before you post a clip publicly, make sure the people in it are okay with that. Don't post clips that reveal someone's home, school, workplace, or other identifying location without their permission.
- **The hider cam sneak peek** takes a photo through the rear camera of a consenting hider and shows it to hunters. Hiders, point your camera responsibly, and don't capture bystanders' faces or private places.

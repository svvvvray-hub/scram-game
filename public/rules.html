🎯 Goal & roles

Hiders 🙈 try to stay uncaught until the clock runs out. Hunters 🔦 try to catch every hider first. The host picks roles (any number of hunters, at least one) or taps 🎲 Randomize.

Hunters never see hiders live — only where they were at each ping. Hunters see each other live. Your own dot is always green.

Infection (on by default): when you're caught you become a hunter, start getting pings, and help hunt the rest.

Catching: tag someone in real life, then confirm it in the app. A hider taps I got caught → the nearest hunter within 30 m (any hunter if there's no GPS) gets a Confirm prompt. Or a hunter taps I caught someone and picks the hider, who confirms. The other side has 20 s to confirm; the host can confirm or cancel any claim. Hunters win when every hider is caught; hiders win if anyone is left when time's up.

Who sees whom (live): hunters see every other hunter live on the map. With the host setting Teammates visible (on by default), hiders who haven't been caught see the other uncaught hiders live (🤝 purple marker with their name, every GPS update, about every 2–5 s). Hunters never see hiders live; they only get the ping snapshots. Caught and infected? You lose the hider view instantly, start seeing hunters, and the hiders stop seeing you. Caught without infection? You become a spectator and see nothing live until the recap.

Lobby size: up to 20 players per lobby.

🎮 Game modes

The host picks a mode in the lobby. New lobbies start in Classic. Pings, the playing field, the chime (where noted) and catch confirmation work in every mode.

  • Classic: pings, field, chime, catch confirmation. No decoys, bounties, power-ups, cam peek or compass.
  • Party: Classic + decoys, bounties, power-ups (Normal) and the compass.
  • Chaos: everything on, power-ups every 90 s.
  • Custom: shows every individual toggle.

Sections below about decoys, bounties, power-ups, cam peek and the compass only apply when that mode (or Custom) has them on.

📱 Playing on your phone
  • Invites: the lobby shows a big join code (tap to copy), a QR code and a Share invite button. Scanning the QR opens the join screen with the code filled in.
  • 📅 Plan the next game: after a round the host can tap Plan the next game on the recap to start a quick poll (a few times + places). Share the link; friends vote with just a name, and the host locks the winner with an Add-to-calendar button.
  • Role card: at the start you get a full-screen card with your role, your goal and the head-start countdown. Tap to close it (hunters' card closes itself at release). You get one when you're infected too.
  • Action bar: everything is in the bar at the bottom: power-up slot (with a cooldown ring, greyed out in sudden death), decoy (badge = charges left), Catch / I got caught, Record, and ⋯ More (sound check, these rules, leave).
  • Pings: a ripple shows on the map where each hider was seen, hunters' phones buzz briefly, and the ring around the ping timer counts down.
  • Tips: short one-time tips pop up the first time something happens. Reset tips (lobby, or ⋯ More) shows them again.
  • Battery saver: when you've moved less than 5 m and nobody you know of (or any drop / bounty) is within 150 m, your phone sends GPS every 15 s instead of every 3-5 s (🔋 Saver in the top bar). 5 s before every ping the server asks every phone for a fresh fix, so pings and chimes stay exact. The map stops drawing while the page is in the background.
⏱ Timing
  • A standard game is 20 minutes (host can change it; all the timings below scale with game length).
  • Head start: 120 s for hiders to run; hunters' maps are covered.
  • Pings every 60 s: hunters see each hider's spot at that moment (with an accuracy circle).
  • Cam peek at minute 10: hunters get one photo from each consenting hider's rear camera (no location).
  • Sudden death from minute 18 (the last 2 min): map pings stop and hunters get a compass that points at their nearest hider, with a hot/cold band. No bounties or power-ups.
🟨 Playing field & out of bounds

The host can draw a boundary: a circle (¼, ½ or 1 mile), a drawn shape, or both (then the field is where they overlap). Outside is shaded dark.

Leave the field and you get a loud warning. A hider who stays out for 30 s is revealed at their true spot on the next ping (decoys don't help).

👻 Decoys

Each hider has 2 decoys per round. Arm one and your next ping shows hunters a fake spot 30–150 m away. Not available in sudden death.

🔔 The chime

At each ping, any hider within 40 m of a hunter chimes: a soft sound plays on the hider's phone, it vibrates and the screen flashes. Hunters only learn "a hider chimed nearby" — no location. So stand still and hide your phone!

The chime is gentle and defaults to 25% volume (we play in neighbourhoods). The host can set 10–100% and test it on every phone; tap 🔔 Check your sound in the lobby. Websites can't override silent mode or your volume.

💰 Bounties

Every 5 min a 💰 appears on everyone's map. Get within 15 m to claim it. A hider who claims it wipes their next ping (hunters see nothing). A hunter who claims it triggers an instant surprise ping of every hider.

🧩 Hider tasks

In Party and Chaos (or Custom with tasks on), hiders get about 3 short phone tasks per game (5 on “Often”). Your phone gives a short buzz and a 🧩 Task button appears. You can hide the card with “✕ Later” and open it again until it expires (about a minute). The timer starts when you open it.

  • 🧮 Quick math: 3 sums in 15 s.
  • 🧩 Slide puzzle: put 1–8 in order (30 s).
  • 🔌 Wire connect: drag each wire to its colour (20 s).
  • 🧘 Hold still: stay within about 8 m for 20 s. The server checks this with your GPS.

Finish it: Extra Time, so the game clock loses 30 s (scaled to game length). Hunters aren’t told when a task is completed.
Fail, run out of time or ignore it: your next ping is noisy. It ignores your cloak or decoy and shows a tight accuracy circle.

🟨 Drawing the boundary

Host: tap ✏️ Draw shape. In 📍 Tap corners mode (the default), tap each corner. Straight lines join them. Use ↩ Undo last point to fix a mistake, then ✅ Close shape. ✍️ Freehand lets you drag a finger around the area instead.

📍 Auto-tag

If a hunter stays within the tag radius of a hider (3 m by default; the host can pick 2 m for strong GPS only, 3 or 5 m, or turn it off) for about 10 seconds in a row, the server tags the hider automatically.

  • Both phones show a ring that fills up. The hider sees “A hunter is tagging you”. The hunter only sees “Closing in…”, with no direction, distance or name.
  • Stand side by side and the tag usually lands in about 10–15 s. The server smooths each phone’s last few GPS fixes and allows for GPS accuracy (range + half of both phones’ accuracy, capped), and the ring keeps filling while most of the last 10 s counted as close. A 3 m/s jog-by or walking past 10 m away never tags.
  • Clearly move apart (more than about 15 m for 3 s) and the ring resets. If a phone’s GPS goes quiet for a moment (up to about 8 s), the ring pauses (⏸) instead of resetting.
  • While a tag is in progress, both phones send GPS every second. Keep the screen on and the app open.
  • If the hider’s GPS stays quiet after the ring passed 30%, the hunter gets a Tap to confirm tag button. The hider is asked “Were you tagged?”. If they don’t answer within 15 s, the host decides.
  • Walking past doesn’t count. It needs continuous contact confirmed by more than one GPS update.
  • With very poor accuracy (worse than about ±25 m), auto-tag pauses and you use the manual buttons.
  • The host can keep or remove the manual Catch / I got caught buttons as a fallback. The host can always confirm a catch.
📍 Regroup after a round

When a round ends, the recap shows a Regroup panel. Everyone sees the room creator’s live position as a “Meet here” pin, how far away they are, and a Head back to host button that opens walking directions. If the creator has left, the current host is the meeting point. This only happens after the round. During a game, nothing changes.

  • Tap I’m in for another round or I’m done for now. If you’re done, you can leave a quick rating and a note for the host, or skip it.
  • The host sees who’s coming back, who’s done and who hasn’t answered, plus each player’s distance, whether they’re getting closer or moving away, and whether their connection dropped. Then they tap Start next round for the players who are in.
  • Optional: 🔔 Ping me for the next game with your @ or an email.
🎁 Power-ups

Drops land on walkable ground (streets, paths and footways from OpenStreetMap, never motorways, never inside buildings). If map data isn’t available, they land at a random spot in the field.

Drops appear every 3 min (host setting: Off / Normal / Chaos = every 90 s), alternating hider drops and hunter drops — none in the first 2 min or in sudden death. They spawn 50–200 m from a player, inside the field, and vanish after 2.5 min.

  • 🎁 Hider drops: only hiders can pick them up. Hunters see where they are as a dimmed icon (not what’s inside), so they’re a place hiders might head for. When one is taken it just disappears. ⚡ Hunter drops are visible to everyone.
  • Pick up: stand within 15 m for 5 s (watch the ring; walking away resets it).
  • Hold one at a time; tap to use. 3-min cooldown between uses. Not usable in sudden death.
  • Stealing: a hider can destroy a hunter drop the same way — but the spot flashes for everyone and that hider is instantly pinged to the hunters at their real location.
  • Everyone sees a toast when one is used: "A hider used Jammer!" (hiders stay anonymous) or "Hunter Murray used Sonar!".

⏺ Recording & the radar bubble

Tap ⏺ Record to film your game. Clips stay on your phone and are never uploaded (unless you choose to send highlights to the host after the game). While recording, the camera fills the screen and the map shrinks into a round radar bubble — tap it to swap camera/map, drag it to any corner.

⚙ Quality: Saver (480p), Standard (720p, default) or High (1080p). Long recordings are saved in 3-min parts.

🏁 Recap & trophies

After the game, everyone sees an animated replay of all paths (trails are never shown during play), a heat map, a timeline, awards and trophies, and can share a recap image. A close call is a hunter within 20 m of a hider who then survived 60 s.

Awards: Longest survivor, Closest call, Most distance walked, Decoy master, Bounty hunter, Fastest catch, Most out of bounds.

Trophies: 👻 Ghost · 🐕 Bloodhound · 🪒 Close Shave · 🎭 Decoy Master · 💰 Bounty Hunter · 🏃 Marathoner · 🪄 Escape Artist · 🩸 First Blood · 🧟 Zombie · 🚧 Rule Bender · 🦝 Thief · ⚡ Power Player

🎬 Highlight reels & Make it viral

If you recorded, your phone automatically builds a highlight reel from your own clips (catches, close calls, decoys, bounties, the final seconds) with captions and a trophy card. Keep the page open while it builds (takes about as long as the reel).

Optionally tap Send my highlights to host to share only your short cuts (max 15 MB, deleted after 30 min) so the host can make a group reel. 🔥 Make it viral makes a vertical 15–30 s clip with big captions and a share link.

⚠️ Safety rules
  • You must be 18+ to play.
  • Play only in public places. Never trespass onto private property, yards or closed areas.
  • Watch for traffic. Don't play near busy roads, and never look at your phone while crossing.
  • Get permission from anyone you record. Recording private conversations without consent can be illegal (e.g. California).
  • Be respectful — keep the chime volume low, especially at night.
